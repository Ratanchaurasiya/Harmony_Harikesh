Technical & Structural Specifications

Designed with high-strength concrete elements, fully tested at RWDI Wind Tunnel facility. Structural engineering utilizes advanced earthquake resistant shear wall technology for paramount safety and lifetime structural guarantees.
Each tower hosts 5 high-speed elevators (including dedicated service units) with rescue capability systems. Electrical substations and emergency generation provide complete power security backup for mechanical utilities.
Equipped with architectural window frame profiles featuring double-glazed glass for thermal and noise reduction. Exterior cladding and texture systems offer lifelong insulation from monsoon and high summer heat loads.
